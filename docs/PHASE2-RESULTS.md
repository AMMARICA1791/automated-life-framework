# Phase 2 Results — Methodological Status

## Scope
Ten hypothetical, non-sensitive decisions were supplied as examples for the three sages. The first five have narrative analyses; decisions 06–10 are summarized in a table.

## Reported observations (not independently validated)
The source document reports 8/10 unconfirmed claims, 9/10 impressions without numerical support, and 10/10 potential contradictions. It further asserts that 7/10 were driven by FOMO or pressure and that the sages caught 90% of impulsive decisions.

**These figures are assertions in the supplied write-up, not measured outcomes from a controlled study.** The decisions were constructed to include prior commitments, so a 10/10 contradiction detection rate does not establish performance on independent decisions. There is no blinded baseline, independent adjudication, prospective user outcome, or validated comparison against simple pros/cons. The source also says three cases represent genuine evolution without identifying and documenting those cases individually.

## What this phase demonstrates
The prompt designs encourage three distinguishable lines of inquiry: source/date, supporting evidence, and consistency with prior decisions. The examples illustrate these lenses, but do not yet establish effectiveness at reducing regret or a 90% detection accuracy.

## Next validation
Use 20–30 independently authored, non-sensitive decisions; compare a plain pros/cons review against the three-sage review; predefine a scoring rubric, blind the assessor to condition, document false positives, and report both quantitative and qualitative findings.

## Privacy
All included scenarios are hypothetical. Automatic Drive publication remains disabled.
